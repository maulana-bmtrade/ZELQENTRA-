# ZELQENTRA — Brand Identity Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0 · 3 November 2026  
**Brand status:** Working identity recommendation; trademark and domain clearance pending.

## 1. Brand foundation

### Brand name
**ZELQENTRA**  
Pronunciation: **zel-KEN-tra**.

### Brand meaning
A coined name created for a global business technology ecosystem. The name is intentionally not a literal dictionary word:
- **Zel** — a distinctive opening sound, chosen to create strong recognition.
- **Qen** — a conceptual reference to connected intelligence and a central network node.
- **Tra** — a subtle reference to trade, trajectory and movement.

These are brand associations created for the identity, not historical linguistic meanings.

### Brand philosophy
Global trade should be easier to understand, evaluate and act on. Zelqentra brings business identity, product discovery, buyer demand and commercial context into one connected environment.

### Positioning
**The global trade intelligence network connecting buyers, suppliers and business partners.**

Zelqentra is positioned above the “online product catalogue” category: a B2B discovery and intelligence layer for sourcing, business relationships and trade workflows.

### Vision
Make global business opportunities easier to discover, evaluate and pursue with clarity and confidence.

### Mission
Connect companies, products, buyer demand and commercial relationships through a trusted, data-aware and scalable digital ecosystem.

### Values
1. **Clarity** — make complex business information understandable.
2. **Trust** — respect identity, evidence, permissions and transparency.
3. **Connection** — bring relevant buyers, suppliers and partners together.
4. **Intelligence** — turn structured information into useful context.
5. **Momentum** — reduce friction from discovery to commercial action.
6. **Global mindset** — design for cross-border business and different markets.

### Brand promise
A clearer path from business discovery to a confident next step.

### Slogan
**Connect with clarity. Trade with confidence.**

### Supporting descriptor
**The intelligence layer for global trade.**

### Brand story
Global commerce is full of fragmented information: suppliers in one place, buyer needs in another, product details in a third and trust signals scattered across documents and conversations. Zelqentra is designed to connect those signals into a clearer business journey. It helps companies present their capabilities, discover relevant opportunities and build relationships with better context. The ambition is not to be another catalogue, but to become an intelligent connection layer for international trade.

## 4. Logo system

### Core concept
The symbol is a custom geometric **Z route** formed by connected horizontal and diagonal strokes with four endpoint nodes. It represents:
- the movement of trade across markets;
- multiple business participants connected by a common route;
- intelligence connecting separate data points;
- a clear path from discovery to opportunity.

The symbol is an original vector construction created for this project. It is not a copied logo or a claim of trademark uniqueness.

### Supplied assets
- `assets/zelqentra-logo.svg` — primary horizontal logo.
- `assets/zelqentra-symbol.svg` — secondary symbol for website header and favicon use.
- `assets/zelqentra-monochrome.svg` — dark monochrome wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `assets/zelqentra-monochrome-white.svg` — white monochrome wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `assets/zelqentra-logo-reversed.svg` — full-colour symbol with white wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `assets/zelqentra-app-icon.svg` — app icon / 512-viewBox SVG.
- `assets/favicon.svg` — browser favicon.
- `assets/zelqentra-social.svg` — social/Open Graph artwork.

### Usage
- Use the primary logo on brand documents and presentations.
- Use the symbol plus wordmark in website headers.
- Use the symbol alone where space is constrained (favicon, app tile, avatar).
- On dark surfaces, use `assets/zelqentra-logo-reversed.svg` or `assets/zelqentra-monochrome-white.svg`.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add unapproved shadows or recolour the gradient independently.
- Keep clear space around the symbol at least equal to the width of one node circle.

### Logo variants
The supplied primary, reversed, symbol, dark/white monochrome, favicon and app-icon SVGs cover the initial system.

### Colour rationale
- Midnight Navy communicates depth, stability and enterprise-grade seriousness.
- Signal Teal adds a distinctive innovation cue without relying on the former lime-green identity.
- Intelligent Blue communicates information, navigation and network intelligence.
- Ice Background and Pure Surface preserve legibility and a restrained premium feel.

### Accessibility
Use dark text on light surfaces and white text on the dark navy surfaces. Do not use Signal Teal as small body text on white; reserve it for emphasis, iconography, focus, borders and larger display elements. Validate all production contrast pairs before launch.

## 6. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / headings | Manrope | 700–800 weight, compact tracking, short line lengths |
| Body / UI | DM Sans | 400–600 weight, high readability |
| Buttons | DM Sans | 700 weight, concise labels |
| Dashboard / data | DM Sans | Tabular figures where available; clear numeric hierarchy |

The project already loads Manrope and DM Sans from Google Fonts. The rebrand retains these fonts and does not add a font dependency.

## 7. Visual language

- **Iconography:** simple geometric line icons, consistent stroke and restrained fills.
- **Illustration:** abstract trade routes, nodes, data paths, maps and network flows; avoid generic shopping-cart imagery as the main brand metaphor.
- **Buttons:** existing compact rectangular controls; 6px radius, clear primary/secondary hierarchy.
- **Cards:** white surfaces, subtle 1px borders, 8px radius and restrained elevation.
- **Modals:** 12px radius; keep focus and readable form spacing.
- **Spacing:** 8px base rhythm, with section spacing based on the existing responsive layout.
- **Shadows:** low-opacity, soft and directional; avoid heavy glow effects.
- **Background pattern:** subtle node/route geometry, low contrast and never behind dense text.
- **Motion:** short, purposeful transitions; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
