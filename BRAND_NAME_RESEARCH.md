# ZELQENTRA — Brand Name Research & Preliminary Screening

**Research date:** 3 November 2026  
**Scope:** 30+ coined/name candidates for a global B2B trade-intelligence platform.  
**Decision status:** Strategic recommendation only — not legal clearance.

## Executive recommendation

**Recommended working brand: ZELQENTRA**  
Pronunciation guide: **zel-KEN-tra** (three syllables).

The general web-index search performed for this naming exercise did not surface an exact indexed result for “ZELQENTRA”. This is a preliminary signal only. It does **not** establish that the name is legally available, that a company can register it, or that any domain or social handle is available.

The name should proceed to professional clearance only after exact, phonetic and similarity searches in the relevant official registers, especially Indonesia, the United States, the EU and the intended first export/import markets.

## Screening method and limits

Candidates were assessed for:
- distinctiveness and memorability;
- international pronunciation and spelling;
- fit with a technology-led B2B trade ecosystem;
- obvious exact-name and close-name results in general web searches;
- visible company, product, software and trademark conflicts.

This was an initial web-index screen, not a comprehensive legal search. Search engines can omit unindexed, recently filed, local, transliterated or non-English records. Some third-party company/trademark aggregators were used to identify leads; any apparent conflict must be confirmed in the relevant official register.

## Why ZELQENTRA is selected

ZELQENTRA is the strongest strategic candidate in this preliminary pool because:
1. It is a coined word rather than a generic marketplace descriptor.
2. It can represent a platform layer, not merely a product listing site.
3. Its sound is firm and international without tying the business to one product category or country.
4. It can extend into products such as Zelqentra Network, Zelqentra Intelligence, Zelqentra Trust and Zelqentra Commerce.
5. No exact indexed result was surfaced in the general web search used for this first-pass review.

**Name construction is conceptual, not a claim of linguistic etymology:** “Zel” is the distinctive opening sound; “Qen” is intended to evoke a connected intelligence node; “tra” hints at trade and trajectory. Together, the word is designed to suggest connected commercial movement powered by intelligence.

### Required clearance workflow before public launch

1. Search WIPO Global Brand Database for `ZELQENTRA`, `ZEL QENTRA`, `ZEL-QENTRA`, phonetic variants and similar visual marks.
2. Search Indonesia PDKI and the official national registers of the first target markets.
3. Search USPTO, EUIPO eSearch/TMview and UKIPO for identical and similar marks.
4. Focus on Nice Classes **9, 35, 38 and 42** (software, marketplace/business services, communications and SaaS/technology), then add classes relevant to any actual financial, logistics or verification services offered.
5. Check company names in Indonesia and planned incorporation jurisdictions.
6. Check domains through a registrar and ICANN/RDAP; then register the chosen domain if legally and commercially appropriate.
7. Check social handles and app store names directly.
8. Have a qualified trademark professional assess similarity, goods/services overlap, territory and filing strategy before investment in launch media.
