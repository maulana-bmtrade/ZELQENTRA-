# ZELQENTRA B2B — Product Foundation Brief

## Product definition
ZELQENTRA B2B is envisioned as a global intelligent business ecosystem connecting companies, products, buyer demand and commercial opportunities through digital commerce infrastructure and AI-assisted discovery.

## Product pillars
1. Global Marketplace
2. Business Network
3. Business Trust
4. Demand Intelligence
5. AI Business Intelligence
6. B2B Commerce

## Target roles
- Buyer
- Supplier
- Business Partner
- Administrator

## MVP 1.0 scope
- International homepage
- Registration and login
- Company profiles
- Business directory
- Product marketplace and categories
- Search and filtering
- Buyer requests / RFQ
- Supplier quotations
- Buyer and supplier dashboards
- Admin dashboard
- Notification center
- Basic business verification

## Membership
- Basic: free foundational access
- Professional: paid advanced business features
- Enterprise: tailored company solutions

## Revenue model candidates
- Premium membership
- Featured products
- Business verification services
- AI subscriptions
- Business intelligence subscriptions
- Enterprise solutions
- Transaction services

## Guiding principles
Mobile-first, global-ready, secure, structured, scalable, API-ready and PWA-ready. Launch in stages; do not imply that a feature is operational until its complete workflow, data handling and security controls have been implemented and tested.

## Release stages
1. Product foundation
2. UI/UX design
3. Website MVP
4. Backend and database
5. Business intelligence
6. Testing and launch
7. Mobile application
8. International expansion


## Zelqentra brand
The public brand is **Zelqentra** — a global trade intelligence network. Slogan: **Connect with clarity. Trade with confidence.** The brand does not change the technical stack, database schema, Supabase project slug or business workflows.
