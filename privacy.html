Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Pre-launch disclosure: This policy is a working disclosure for the current ZELQENTRA website. Before public commercial launch, the platform operator's full legal name, business address, applicable jurisdiction and any additional processing partners must be confirmed and added here. This page is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the ZELQENTRA website, create an account, or submit business information through features made available on the platform. ZELQENTRA is being developed as a business discovery and B2B networking platform. Contact the site team at maulana.bmtrade@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

2. Information that may be collected

  • Account information: name, business email address, authentication identifiers and account metadata you submit.
  • Business information: company name, business role, profile details, product information and business enquiries you choose to provide.
  • Technical information: browser/device information, basic diagnostic events and connection logs generated by hosting or service providers.
  • Communications: information included when you contact the team by email or WhatsApp.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, display business profiles and listings, support buyer/supplier workflows, respond to enquiries, maintain platform security, troubleshoot errors and improve the service. Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or notice.

4. Hosting, authentication and database services

The current application is configured to use Supabase for authentication and database functions. When those features are enabled and used, information submitted through the relevant forms may be processed by the Supabase project configured for this website. The website may also use its hosting provider and content-delivery services to serve pages and protect availability. Their own terms and privacy documentation apply to their services. No payment processing is represented as active in this release.

5. Public business information

Some business profile or product information may be designed for public display so buyers and suppliers can discover one another. Do not submit personal, confidential, financial or sensitive information into public profile or product fields. Only information intended for public business discovery should be published.

6. Cookies and session storage

Authentication features may use browser storage and session technologies to keep you signed in, refresh sessions and protect account access. Your browser settings can restrict storage, but doing so may prevent login or other features from working.

7. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, disclosed when required by law, or shared when necessary to protect users and platform security. ZELQENTRA should not sell personal information as a business model. Publicly submitted business listings may be visible to other visitors according to the feature's design and access rules.

8. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above or required by law. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are intended, including managed authentication and database access policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Contact maulana.bmtrade@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes or secret keys by email or chat.

10. Children

This business platform is intended for business users and is not designed for children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as platform features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
